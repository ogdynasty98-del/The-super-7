## Smokin’ Buds 360 connector

The Android app in `app/` requests a connection to the paired Smokin’ Buds 360 at
Bluetooth address `E8:EF:16:21:70:E3`.

1. Build and install the app on an Android device.
2. Pair the earbuds in **Settings > Connected devices > Pair new device**.
3. Open the app and grant nearby-device access.
4. Tap **Connect to Smokin’ Buds 360**.

Android does not allow an app to bypass pairing or guarantee a connection. The
earbuds must be paired first, and the phone may still require the user to
approve or retry the connection.
